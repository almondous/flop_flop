YELLOW PAPER / MONEY DESTINATIONS

85 to compute.15 to validation.

FLOP Labs' October 25 public mirror sync changes the draft's account of who receives the money: inference payments, transaction fees, penalties and passive staking now have clearer destinations. The document remains 0.5.0 draft.

The new commit, 5730f4b, extends the public record beyond our October 13 report. It brings D-0525 and D-0529–D-0531 into the visible decision record and aligns the main text and canonical parameters. The decisions carry earlier ratification dates; the fresh event is their publication in this mirror.

Transaction fees

D-0529100% to the block author

The published parameter changes from 10% transaction-fee burn to 0%. Base, length and weight fees, plus tips, go to the author. Zero burn changes the destination; it does not make transactions free.

Slashed collateral

D-0529100% to the Foundation

The draft replaces legacy “full-burn” language with confiscation and transfer to FLOPFoundationAccount. A full slash still takes the penalized stake; the text says the proceeds are transferred rather than destroyed.

Referral incentives

D-0531Off-chain reserve funding

On-chain referral commissions are superseded by an explorer leaderboard and periodic lotteries funded from the 800 million FLOP genesis reserve, which also serves other ecosystem programmes. This is a specification of the programme, not a payout announcement.

D-0530 / TWO DIFFERENT ROLES

Operate with your stake.
Lock without an operator.

Operator self-stake and community staking are separate paths.

The draft prohibits third-party delegation into validator or miner stakes for a share of operator rewards. Delegation calls, caps and commissions are marked deprecated.

Non-operating holders instead have a duration-locked community pool assigned the 5% block-reward leg. D-0530 describes seven tiers with no operator-slashing exposure. Exact tier weights and claim mechanics remain under E.40; the text does not establish an available product or a guaranteed yield.

D-0528 separately permits sponsor-owned registration bonds on devnet and testnet. They count as self-stake only when the runtime enables that feature, and refunds return to the sponsor. Mainnet questions remain open.
