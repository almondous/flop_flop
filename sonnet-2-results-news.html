What became official

The official repository now separates the stages that previously looked inconsistent: judges read the five highest counted poems, while the three highest-voted eligible poems were the finalists under the published rules. The winner was selected from those three.

The release also turns a contest announcement into an auditable settlement. It publishes standings for 76 eligible entries and nine ineligible entries, the frozen winning poem, a recipient-by-recipient allocation file, the canonical payout map and the referee’s settlement receipt.

There are 6,856 recipient DIDs in total. Claims do not open until mainnet is live. At that point a signed claim chooses the payment destination; it does not alter the amount recorded in this result.
