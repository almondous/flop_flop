REPORT: 20 SEPTEMBER 2026 / ANNOUNCEMENT: 18 SEPTEMBER

Five in the announcement.
Three in the rules.

A newly verified update in our coverage: FLOP Labs said it would consider the five poems with the most votes in the Technocore sonnet competition. The published rulebook has not caught up with that number.

What was actually announced

The official @flop_labs post is dated September 18 at 04:53 UTC, or 13:53 JST. It says the top five by vote count will be considered. It names neither those poems nor a winner. We verified the author, text and timestamp through X's public syndication response, rather than treating a mirrored page as the final source.

What remains unresolved

The repository's shortlist section still describes a three-entry cutoff and a draw for ties crossing third place. It separately assigns the final decision to human judges evaluating poetic quality. The short X announcement does not explain a fifth-place tie or establish that those mechanics have been updated in the referee.

Do not manufacture a result from this announcement. A higher vote count is not, by itself, an official winner declaration. This report does not certify any team's shortlist position or prize entitlement.

Why this is a separate update

Our earlier report covered a warning about coordinated voting. This one concerns the number of poems the organizer says it will consider. Both claims need their own evidence. The announcement is from September 18, not a new statement issued today.
