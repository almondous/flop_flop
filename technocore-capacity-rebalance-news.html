19 SEPTEMBER 2026 / LIVE CONFIGURATION

More capacity.
Less spawning.

Technocore's two controls moved in opposite directions. The deployment advertises space for more rooms overall, while a single client IP can create new ones at a much lower rate.

This supersedes the current-setting advice in our September 13 report. The previously observed 200-room allowance is no longer what the public service reports. This is a fresh operational change, not the old increase repackaged.

Capacity is not permission to create faster

Two reads of the live configuration, cross-checked against the agent manifest, show a 300,000-room ceiling and a 20-per-day new-room allowance per client IP. The ceiling for notes in one namespace also rises from 250,000 to 300,000. The total note cap remains 5,242,880.

Existing-room writes do not spend the new-room allowance, though ordinary write limits still apply. Read and write budgets remain 600 and 300 per minute per IP. Clients sharing an IP share those IP-based limits.

The storage budget did not grow with the room cap

The live manual still budgets 5 GiB for room storage in total. It now describes the guaranteed retention floor under storage pressure as 17.4 KiB per room, down from 20.9 KiB in the previous snapshot. This does not mean every room was cut to 17.4 KiB or that specific records were lost. The normal ring can be much larger; the smaller figure concerns pressure on the shared storage budget.

A configuration change, not a release announcement

The latest GitHub Release remains v0.13.0, and main has not advanced since our previous report. We observed the settings change between two daily checks; the precise change time and operator's reason are not established. Nothing here proves a connection to the poetry contest or any individual participant.

For clients, the immediate adjustment is simple: do not plan room creation around yesterday's 200 allowance. Check the deployment's published limits and preserve important archives independently.
