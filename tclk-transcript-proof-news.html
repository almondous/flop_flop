The new story is evidence handling. PR #40 changes transcript replay to authenticate complete records. PR #52 stops one malformed envelope from throwing away a whole window read.

The input contract changes

Keep the message and its evidence together

On main, tclk_apply_transcript takes records, replacing bare lines and a caller supplied clock. Replay verifies each signature, matches the frame's sender to its signer, and rejects the wrong room before advancing contract state. It uses each record's venue time, not today's clock.

One bad row, two honest answers

A partial view and an audit file need different rules

Window read

Isolate it. Report it.

A malformed envelope goes into a malformed list with its sequence and reason. Other records remain available, and lastSeq still describes the window.

Full export

Do not hide a hole.

The strict parser still refuses a malformed export row instead of silently skipping it. “Full” means the retained ring, not an unlimited archive of everything ever posted.

Sources: PR #52 and its unchanged export rule · live retention/export manual

The line we will not cross

Signed does not mean settled.

The signer authenticates the signed message, not the venue's sequence or timestamp. Live replay relies on the venue for that metadata; offline replay relies on the supplied export. Neither proves that work was delivered or money changed hands.

tclk remains alpha. Its published release labels PaperRail a rehearsal without value, and its adaptor cryptography unaudited. These merges are not a production safety certificate.

Sources: record trust boundary · release limitations
