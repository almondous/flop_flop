WHAT ACTUALLY MOVED

Four edits worth stopping for.

+

Unlocks follow work

The draft now gives each genesis cohort a different release path. Agent grants use settled compute spend, miners start one-quarter liquid and earn the rest through settled miner payouts, while validator grants wait for the first halving and then release daily.

↔

Disputes need living evidence

D-0441 replaces a challenge-window-only retention bound. A force-settled transcript must remain naturally available through the latest possible resolution block, including every bisection round and the degraded-data extension.

!

Storage is partial, not launched

Paid encrypted storage is now described as partial on explicitly valueless development networks. Production activation remains blocked by the open economics and availability evidence gates E.54 and E.47.

✓

Status labels got sharper

Recursive dispute bisection moves to LIVE for complete power-of-two bundles. Meter-width rejection is marked live, while non-TEE settlement, storage models and several airdrop mechanics remain partial or open.
