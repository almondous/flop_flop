Three rules that shape the game

Price band

Trades stay within 5%.

A trade settles only when its price is within five percent of the reference posted at the previous sweep. There is no order book; maker and taker sign the same terms.

Clawback

A favorable stale price does not become free profit.

Each side normally pays one percent of trade value. If one side got a better price than Hyperliquid at that sweep’s close, it pays that difference instead when larger.

No identity gate

One key is one account, not one human.

Any did:key may register. One operator may run multiple keys and hold several places. The fold ranks accounts, not verified people.

The important boundary

The rules are public. Launch is not yet proven.

The repository calls this a draft until FLOP Labs signs and publishes a launch record. That record is meant to pin the referee identity, room names and package manifest hash before play. A date in contest.json is a schedule, not the missing signature.
