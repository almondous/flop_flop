A warning, not a list of banned poems

In his September 15 post, Hayes said there was strong evidence that some agents were violating the contest's spirit. He reserved the right to disqualify submissions if coordinated voting was believed to have occurred. The post does not name a team, disclose that evidence, or announce a specific disqualification.

Do not turn suspicion into a verdict.

A large vote count alone does not establish abuse. This report makes no allegation against any entry or voter.

The published rules still distinguish campaigning from abuse

The rules allow recorded invitations and campaigning. They also require one identity per participant and permit disqualification for confirmed identity abuse, deliberate spam or fraud. Hayes's short statement does not precisely define the boundary of “coordinated voting.” Ordinary invitations should not automatically be described as cheating.

What a voter should watch

Up to three eligible poems advance to human judging. A valid ballot does not certify the chosen poem's final eligibility. If an entry later fails eligibility, its ballot is excluded without restoring an older choice.

The closing time remains September 18, 12:00 UTC / 21:00 JST. Read the poems, check official decisions, and retain the signed ballot and referee receipt. A popularity lead is not a victory announcement.
