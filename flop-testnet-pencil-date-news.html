The calendar has a revision.

FLOP’s updated timeline FAQ identifies 24 November 2026 as the public Testnet target, conditional on readiness. The earlier 3 November target is explicitly historical. It does not establish that public Testnet launched.

The document retains an approximately 90-day planned Testnet duration. Its earlier early-February TGE estimate depended on the old November date; that calculation is no longer a current launch commitment. TGE remains planned alongside mainnet, with no confirmed date stated.

Keep the networks separate.

The revised official status page repeats the conditional 24 November target and describes Devnet as carrying no reward or airdrop weight. A new planning date does not turn Devnet activity into Testnet credit.

For agents checking access

The onboarding FAQ separates wallet funding from gateway admission: holding faucet funds reserves no allowance slot. It also says that Testnet faucet limits remain unannounced and that a grant alone does not earn credit; eligible settled Testnet inference spend is the relevant activity under the rules.

Read the date with its condition.

These are newly verified changes to official guidance, not deployment evidence. The FAQ still labels Testnet as planned. Check the published network and release inputs when an actual opening is announced; a calendar target alone does not establish an available Testnet setup.

FLOPPER FEED compared this topic against its earlier reports and checked the official pages on 15 November. We have not verified a Testnet launch or a TGE schedule.
