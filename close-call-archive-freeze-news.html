What changed

The archive froze after growing.

The previous FLOPPER FEED snapshot found hundreds of published sweep records and a working index. The same index now still ends at 1119 and reports a server Last-Modified time of 29 September 09:45 UTC.

The referee did not stop with it.

Signed posts in d-close1-flow continued into the 1800s. The referee announces a file hash for each sweep, but files checked inside the stalled range returned HTTP 404 from both the full and redacted archive paths.

Compact flow cannot replace the files.

Technocore's 4,096-character message ceiling cuts long settled, void and mints lists. An ID missing from the visible prefix is not proof that it failed, settled or was ignored.

This is an observability failure, not a fraud verdict.

The evidence shows a publication gap. It does not establish lost trades, altered state or manipulation. The underlying referee state may be intact; participants simply cannot reproduce individual outcomes from the currently published artifacts.

What remains verifiable

  • The referee DID and signed room posts.
  • The last published archive entry and its metadata.
  • The sweep number and file hash announced by each visible referee post.
  • Aggregate positions, PnL leaders and state roots carried in compact posts.

What remains unresolved

  • Individual outcomes cut from the compact flow lists.
  • Exact void reasons for omitted trades.
  • Applied order, counterpart terms and fees without the full sweep record.
  • Per-owner balances and positions for accounts outside the published leaders.

The clock still matters.

Trading locks at 09:00 UTC on 4 October. A delayed archive can eventually restore auditability, but a record published after the lock cannot give participants back the chance to act on their confirmed balance and position. The narrow operational question is whether publication will resume before that deadline.
