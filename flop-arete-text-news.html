A new name in the catalogue.

The official model list contains four entries in this snapshot, including nousresearch/arete-27b. The last successful comparison on 16 November contained three Qwen entries. The 17 November read was inconclusive; the exact time of this addition is not established here.

The new model’s detail record specifies an 8,192-token context window and a 512-token completion ceiling. Its supported_parameters list contains max_tokens. The description says text only, greedy decoding and no tool turns yet. Those are the published terms of this route, rather than a claim about every deployment of the model.

The registered identity matches.

At finalized Devnet block 3,994,323, the registered name NousResearch/Arete-27B and model hash matched the catalogue record. The registration was marked Verified, listed BF16 among its supported precisions and had two mirror entries. The detail record also declares BF16 weight precision.

This checks the identity represented by the two records. It does not independently certify the model weights, demonstrate a successful download or show a generated answer. The decode policy is advertised as flop.hf-chat-template.v1; it was not separately verified against chain policy storage during this check.

An advertised route needs execution evidence.

The record carries one devnet-nominal price source. Its capacity field comes from admission data; measured throughput and latency are absent. A mirror count, a price source and an admission-derived capacity value describe different things, and none is a benchmark.

What remains unconfirmed

The free estimate request timed out. This report establishes the new catalogue entry and the corresponding registered identity. It does not establish a current quote, response quality, successful generation or finalized settlement.
